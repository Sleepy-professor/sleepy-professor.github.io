How sources are checked

Every lecture is written with AI assistance. Each factual claim is then listed on its lecture page with a named source and locator. We mark how far each has been checked:

Quotation checked

The quoted words were matched against the text of the source.

Source located

The work and edition were identified; the exact passage has not yet been matched line by line.

From source pack

Drawn from a source text we hold; not yet matched line by line.

Awaiting a copy

The source is named; the passage is still to be checked against a copy of the edition.

Where a claim is marked Awaiting a copy, treat it as a lead to check, not as settled. Quotations from editions still in copyright are kept short and attributed. We correct errors when they are reported, and the page for the lecture is updated.

The lectures are interpretation of the sources, not reproductions of them. Fiction (such as Jekyll and Hyde) is described as fiction, and later commentary is labelled as later.

Narration is an AI voice and illustrations are AI-generated. Scripts are written with AI assistance. For learning and reflection, not therapy or medical advice.
